Proposed pilot

Six weeks to a decision based on your own conversations

Weeks 1 to 2

Connect and measure

Login, invoices, and helpdesk wired to the four tools. A test set from a few hundred real past conversations.

Weeks 3 to 4

Shadow mode

The agent drafts, your staff decide what is sent. Configure scoring and human review.

Weeks 5 to 6

Launch decision

Limited release only if agreed quality, access, privacy, and operating checks pass. Otherwise continue shadow mode.

The bar, set by you before we start

  • Purchase questions resolved without a person
  • Quality score on your test set
  • Zero cross-account access in testing
  • Cost per resolved conversation

The ask

A support owner, an engineering owner, and a working session next week to pick the test conversations and set the bar.
